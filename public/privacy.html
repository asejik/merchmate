Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

In short: MerchMate keeps your business data on your own phone or computer. We do not have accounts, we do not run a server that stores your invoices or clients, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

1. What MerchMate is

MerchMate is a free invoicing app for small businesses that runs in your web browser and can be installed on your home screen. You use it to create invoices, quotes and receipts, track payments and orders, and keep a list of clients.

2. What is stored, and where

Everything you enter is saved only on the device you use, in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB and localStorage):

  • your business details: name, address, phone, email, logo, bank account details, brand colour and invoice settings;
  • your clients: names, phone numbers, email and addresses;
  • your invoices, quotes, payments, receipts, private job costs and saved items.

This information is not sent to us. We cannot see it, recover it, or delete it for you. If you clear your browser data, uninstall the app, or lose the device, it is gone unless you saved a backup.

3. Who is responsible for your clients' information

You are. The names and phone numbers of your clients are personal information that you collect and use to run your business, and you decide what to do with it. Because it stays on your device, MerchMate does not process it on your behalf. If data protection law applies to your business (for example the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023), you remain responsible for meeting it, such as keeping your device secure and deleting clients who ask you to.

4. What leaves your device

Only what you choose to send:

  • Invoices, receipts and statements you share as a PDF, image or WhatsApp text go to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp), using your phone's share sheet or a wa.me link. That app's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Backup files you save are created on your device. They are not encrypted and contain your clients and invoices. Keep them somewhere private.
  • Problem reports you create are a text file you can read before sharing. They contain the app version, your device and browser type, storage status, the number of clients and invoices, and recent error messages. They contain no client names, phone numbers, amounts or bank details. Nothing is sent unless you share the file.

5. Hosting and technical logs

The app files are delivered by Vercel, our hosting provider. Like any website host, Vercel may record technical information about requests, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request, to run and protect the service. We do not combine this with any business data, because we do not have any. See Vercel's privacy policy.

6. Cookies and tracking

MerchMate does not use cookies, advertising, analytics or third-party trackers. It uses browser storage and a service worker only to save your data and to make the app work offline.

7. Your choices

  • Export: Settings → Backup & Restore saves everything to a file, and Reports can export CSV files.
  • Delete: remove individual clients and invoices in the app, or clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove everything from the device.
  • Protect: Settings → Data Protection shows whether your browser will keep your data when space runs low. On iPhone and iPad, install the app to your Home Screen, because Safari can erase the data of websites that are not installed.

8. Children

MerchMate is meant for businesses and is not directed at children.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Material changes will be announced in the app.
